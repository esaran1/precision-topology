# Item #8: the distance claim, made pathwise

Junyu's theory review item **#8** (optional). Artifacts: `pathwise_distance.csv`,
`src/pathwise_distance.py`. 400 Phase 2b runs sampled at random from six `a`
values, full recorded trajectories.

---

## What changed

`basin_distances.csv` records the distance from the **initialisation only** — a
single point. The claim that trained solutions are far from the constructed ones
therefore rested on where runs *start*, not on where they *go*.

This computes, for each run, the **minimum over its entire recorded trajectory**
of the distance to the nearest constructed `|w₂| = 1` solution.

**The reference set is verified, not assumed.** For each `a`, constructed
solutions are generated by the recipe (place the **inner** window in the dip,
`b₁ = m₀ = π + arccos(1/a)`, centre `b₂` in the achieved gap) and each candidate
is **confirmed by exact extrema** (`separates()`) before entering the set. Between
**2,034 and 2,768** verified solutions per `a`.

*(A first implementation put the **outer** window in the dip and produced zero
verified solutions. Checked against the recorded `a = 1.25` solution from
`box_emptiness_correction.md`, whose `b₁` equals `m₀` exactly — the inner window
goes in the dip. Corrected before any distance was computed.)*

## Result

| quantity | median | IQR |
|---|---:|---|
| distance at **initialisation** | 5.2234 | [4.8458, 5.4609] |
| **minimum along the whole path** | **4.8773** | [4.6545, 5.2671] |
| distance at the **end** | 49.7288 | [19.8920, 109.6684] |

**Median ratio min/init = 0.9918.** Over an entire trajectory, a run gets at most
**0.8% closer** to the nearest constructed solution than it was at
initialisation — and then departs to **~50** away, an order of magnitude further.

- The minimum occurs at a median step of **27**, and at step 0 in **0 of 400**
  runs: the small approach is an early transient, not the destination.
- 286 of 400 runs have a minimum below their init distance at all; for the rest
  the closest approach *is* effectively the start.

**Solved and unsolved runs are indistinguishable in closest approach**:

| | n | min distance | final distance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| solved | 299 | 4.9476 | 61.9797 |
| unsolved | 101 | 4.8048 | 12.5853 |

Solved runs end up **further** from the constructed solutions (62 vs 13), because
solving requires large `|w₂|` while the constructed family sits at `|w₂| = 1`.

Consistent across `a` (ratio 0.987–0.996 at every value).

## What this supports

The distance claim is now **pathwise**: trained solutions are not merely far from
the constructed ones at their endpoints — **no point on any sampled trajectory
comes meaningfully closer**. Gradient descent does not pass near the analytic
construction on its way anywhere.

This strengthens the existing statement rather than replacing it, and it closes
the obvious objection that a single-endpoint distance might miss a near-approach
mid-training.

**Scope**: 400 runs at six `a` values from the Phase 2b checkpoint set; distances
are Euclidean in the 4-parameter space, with the constructed set restricted to
`|w₂| = 1`. A constructed family at larger `|w₂|` would sit closer to the
endpoints by construction, so the claim is specifically about the `|w₂| = 1`
analytic solutions.
