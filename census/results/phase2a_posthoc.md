## Caveats

- The O(ε ln ε) next term is [KS01] Remark 2.11 (citing [MR80]) for planar folds, reported there as a remark; its extension to this n-dimensional reduction is not proved (math note §14.5). The fit uses it as the theory-motivated form only.
- The crossing is a finite-threshold event (ρ₂ passes q), not the blow-up time; that shifts r by O(ε), which the 3-parameter variant (+ D ε) absorbs. ε̂_F carries the forecaster's own ṡ-extrapolation error; the observed ε_F is a finite-difference estimate from four first-passage steps.
- ε̂_F ∝ ρ to within a slowly varying factor, so 27 rates over 5 octaves constrain two smooth terms well but a third weakly (see the 3-parameter row).
