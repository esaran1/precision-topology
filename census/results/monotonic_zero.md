## The bound, stated for recomputation

- Observed: **k = 0** separations in **n = 5,580** distinct monotonic
  width-3 runs.
- One-sided exact (Clopper–Pearson / rule-of-three form) 95% upper
  bound: the largest p with (1 − p)ⁿ ≥ 0.05, i.e.
  **p ≤ 1 − 0.05^(1/5580) = 5.37 × 10⁻⁴ = 0.0537%.**
- Per-cell: for any single 80-run cell, the same formula gives
  1 − 0.05^(1/80) = 3.68%.

mixture of link families, depths, and protocols — the bound applies to
the mixture, not to each condition; all strata share Adam/Kaiming
ancestry except the protocol stratum; eval is 2,000 points per run, so
